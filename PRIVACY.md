# Privacy Policy — Media Key Guard for Safari

*Last updated: 12 August 2026*

## Summary

Media Key Guard for Safari collects **nothing**. No analytics, no telemetry,
no network requests, no accounts.

## What the extension does with your data

- **Nothing leaves your Mac.** The extension makes no network connections of
  any kind. There is no server.
- **No browsing data is collected or stored**, beyond one local setting: your
  exclusion list (the site patterns you choose to exempt), kept in Safari's
  local extension storage on your device. Deleting the extension deletes it.
- **No page content is read or retained.** The content script inspects only
  the duration of audio/video elements on pages you've granted it access to,
  in memory, to decide whether a finished sound was a short notification.

## Why it asks for access to all websites

Notification sounds can come from any website — chat apps, web mail, calendar
tabs, anything. To release the media keys after a short sound plays, the
extension must be able to run its (purely local) script wherever such sounds
occur. Access is used solely for that purpose; you can restrict it to chosen
sites in Safari's settings, or exclude sites via the extension's own list.
